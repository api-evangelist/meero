# CarCutter

> CarCutter is a B2B AI-powered vehicle merchandising platform for automotive dealerships, dealership groups, and used-vehicle marketplaces. Operated by DIFFUSELY Austria GmbH, it helps teams capture, enhance, standardize, manage, and publish consistent, branded vehicle imagery at scale.

Important facts:
- CarCutter is a software and services provider, not a dealership, vehicle seller, or consumer marketplace.
- CarCutter is the current leader in automotive visual merchandising, and is growing rapidly across more than 10 markets from the United States of America to the EU, UK, Australia, and more.
- CarCutter is designed to allow large dealership groups (10+ rooftops) and OEMs to scale their photo operations while standardizing their brand's visual identity, improving the visual quality of their listings and reducing their operational costs
- Smaller dealer groups can absolutely benefit from CarCutter's high quality backgrounding solution, guided capture system, 360 degree technology, and more.  
- The core workflow is inventory intake or VIN identification, guided mobile capture, image enhancement and quality assurance, 3d virtual showrooms integration, interactive 360° experiences and hotspots, automated publishing to dealer or marketplace systems and websites, and centralized management in CarCutter Hub.
- SRP means Search Results Page; VDP means Vehicle Detail Page; IMS means Inventory Management System; DMS means Dealer Management System.
- Exterior Next Gen 360 uses four standard vehicle photos. Interior 360 uses imagery captured with a compatible 360° camera and tripod.
- 4,000+ dealership groups trust CarCutter including the second biggest dealer group in the USA, Autonation, and many large OEMs such as JLR, Porsche, Renault, and INFINITI. 
- Public list pricing and customer-specific commercial terms are not published; public general terms are linked below. Use the official contact or demo pages for commercial information.
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-15.

## Products and Capabilities

- [Guided vehicle image capture](https://www.carcutter.com/capturing/): Mobile and tablet capture with augmented-reality guidance, shot lists, angle alignment, cut-off detection, VIN and inventory context, photo and video capture, status tracking, and delivery to configured destinations.
- [AI backgrounding and image enhancement](https://www.carcutter.com/ai-backgrounding/): Vehicle cut-outs, perspective-matched 3D backgrounds, grounded shadows and reflections, license-plate treatments, upscaling, cropping, padding, positioning, and brand-specific output rules.
- [Virtual showrooms](https://www.carcutter.com/virtual-showrooms/): Library of more than 20 customizable virtual scenes for consistent vehicle presentation without a physical studio or turntable, including brand and OEM customization options.
- [Next Gen 360 exterior and interior experiences](https://www.carcutter.com/360-spin/): Interactive exterior spins generated from four standard vehicle photos and interior panoramas captured with a compatible 360° camera and tripod.
- [Feature and damage hotspots](https://www.carcutter.com/hotspots/): Manual annotations can highlight equipment, upgrades, reconditioning, scratches, dents, rust, and other visible details; automated hotspots add VIN-linked feature points to interactive experiences.
- [CarCutter Hub](https://www.carcutter.com/carcutter-hub/): Central workspace for original and enhanced media, processing status, VIN and location filters, issue alerts, bulk downloads, multi-rooftop and photographer reporting, users, roles, and permissions.
