What Is a Virtual Car Showroom? A Dealer’s Guide to AI Background Environments TL;DR: A virtual car showroom uses AI to replace the background behind a vehicle photo with a branded, consistent scene—no physical photo booth required. It shifts spend from a $40k–$100k CapEx booth to software OpEx and applies one look across every rooftop.
